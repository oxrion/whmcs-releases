# Oxrion module for WHMCS

Sell [Oxrion](https://oxrion.com/) PHP protection plans as WHMCS products. Each service
becomes a customer in your Oxrion partner account; plans are paid from your partner balance.

**Download:** the newest release on the right, under **Releases**. It is also in your Oxrion
partner panel under **Integrations**.

Requires WHMCS 8 and PHP 8.1 or later. The install guide is in the zip (`README.md`) and in
your partner panel under **Docs > WHMCS module**.
